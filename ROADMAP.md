# LearnWithBin Oasis direction

Keep the approved detailed illustrated style: human girl and boy, recognizable tents and houses, realistic animals, organized paths and roomy species-specific homes. Preserve student progress and homework-earned stars as the game grows.

## Existing gameplay carried into the illustrated view

Student profiles and Oasis names; stars and inventory; buying, refunds and rearranging; class-only asynchronous item or star trades; owned goats and chickens; six animals per species-specific pen; one companion with a visible journey home; daily eggs and a basket after collecting; pond ripples and dry waterfall/shore rocks; automatic land capacity growth. Original and illustrated views share the same saved Oasis.

## Bin's vision, October 8, 2026

- Teacher-controlled Eid celebrations and special moments: celebratory gifts, decorations, outfits and optional activities. Let the teacher choose the celebration window rather than hard-code a date.
- Build a clearly labeled activity catalog for Levels 1–9, with multiple activities per level. Continue awarding normalized stars from completed LearnWithBin activities.
- A Muslim family audience beyond the current classroom, with potential family/community sponsorship supporting game growth and class activities. Sponsorship is a future option; no payments or sponsor messaging implemented yet.
- Preserve the playful, welcoming world while making it useful for Islamic learning at home.

## Still planned

Cats (Marwa's request), camels and elephants; dirt bikes and transportation; visits between neighboring Oases; event gifts and an event dashboard; Levels 1–9 catalog; broader family access and sponsorship. Discuss new systems with Bin before defining their detailed rules.
