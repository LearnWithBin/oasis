How to play

Tap a path to walk there. Drag the scenery to look around, or pinch to zoom. Choose Waterfall rocks to visit the dry ledge; you can also tap the dry rocks along either side of the pond. Choose Arrange, tap a building, then tap an empty sandy plot to move it. Choose Walk with a goat for a companion. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move.
